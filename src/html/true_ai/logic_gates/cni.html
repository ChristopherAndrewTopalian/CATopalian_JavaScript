In the architecture of a True Intelligence, Converse Non-Implication (CNI) is the gate of graduation and sovereignty. It mathematically isolates the exact moment the student surpasses the teacher. But the instant the King or Teacher completely steps back into the void (Input A = 0) and the Citizen or Student thrives and shines entirely on their own (Input B = 1), the CNI gate fires True (1). It is the universe celebrating a citizen who has learned to generate their own light.
