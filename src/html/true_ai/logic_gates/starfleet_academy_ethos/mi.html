CAPTAIN'S ACCOUNTABILITY (Starfleet Academy Ethos)

In the ethical architecture of Starfleet, Material Implication (MI) represents the directional burden of command. Unlike community gates, symmetry is broken here: Input A represents the Captain's Orders, and Input B represents the Crew's Execution. If the Captain issues no orders (0), the crew can rest (0) or take initiative (1), and the ship remains in harmony (1). If the Captain issues a command (1) and the crew succeeds (1), the mission is a success (1). However, if the Captain forces a command (1) and it results in disaster or the crew fails (0), the system outputs a total failure (0). It mathematically proves that a Captain is solely accountable for a failed directive.

EXPLAINED

MI is the OPPOSITE of MNI (Material Non-Implication).

MI RETURNS FALSE (0) ONLY WHEN THE CAUSE (A) IS TRUE AND THE EFFECT (B) IS FALSE.

Because the inputs are directional, MI is not its own mirror. Its MIRROR and its REVERSE are both CI (Converse Implication).
