Vascular Engineering

To understand the danger of a morning Cinnamon Bun and a Coffee, we must look at our biological plumbing. High glucose turns our blood into a thick "Syrup State." Caffeine and Energy Drinks act as Artery Shrinkers. Forcing thick syrup through a shrunken pipe requires our heart to pump at dangerous pressures, damaging the walls of our entire vascular system.
